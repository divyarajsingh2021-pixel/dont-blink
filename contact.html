Frequently Addressed Inquiries

❓ Why does my reaction time vary across different devices?
Different monitors have varying refresh rates (60Hz = 16.6ms frame time, 144Hz = 6.9ms, 240Hz = 4.1ms) and display response times. Additionally, wireless mice and touchscreens add 10-40ms of hardware latency compared to wired optical peripherals.
❓ Can I use DON'T BLINK benchmarks for research or classroom studies?
Yes! You are welcome to use DON'T BLINK for educational, academic, and non-commercial psychomotor demonstrations. Please cite our platform URL: dont-blink-two.vercel.app.
❓ How are high scores saved?
All personal bests, floor streaks, and leaderboard entries are stored locally on your device's browser using localStorage. No personal profile or account creation is necessary.
