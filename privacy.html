Privacy Policy

At DON'T BLINK (accessible from dont-blink-two.vercel.app), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by DON'T BLINK and how we use it.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

DON'T BLINK does not require user accounts, passwords, or personal identity registration to access our games and benchmarks. We do not collect names, home addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses unless you voluntarily contact us via email.

2. Browser Local Storage (Device-Only)

To provide high-score tracking, personal best records, and mute preferences across games, DON'T BLINK utilizes your browser's client-side localStorage API. This data stays 100% on your personal device and is never transmitted to our servers or shared with third parties. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser cache and local storage.

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

DON'T BLINK displays advertisements powered by Google AdSense to support free, open-access development. Google is a third-party vendor on our site and uses cookies to serve ads to visitors based on their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Log Files & Analytics

Like most standard web hosting services, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of this information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement around the website, and gathering demographic information.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data that a business has collected about consumers, and request that a business delete any personal data collected. Because DON'T BLINK does not sell personal information or collect personally identifying information, our practices inherently align with CCPA protections.

6. GDPR Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the right to access, the right to rectification, the right to erasure, the right to restrict processing, the right to object to processing, and the right to data portability. If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you.

7. Children's Information

Protecting children's privacy online is especially important. DON'T BLINK does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

8. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through our Contact Page.
