⚡ ABOUT THE ARCADE

Biological Benchmark Methodology

Human visual reaction time is primarily governed by optical transmission from the retina to the visual cortex, followed by neuromuscular transmission to motor neurons.

In our game, percentile comparisons are calculated using empirical biological distribution models: Mean = 273ms and Standard Deviation = 45ms based on global reaction-time studies and hardware latency baselines.

Display & Input Latency

Measured reaction times include display refresh latency (16.6ms on 60Hz displays, 6.9ms on 144Hz displays) and input polling delays. For maximum performance, use a high refresh-rate display and wired input.
