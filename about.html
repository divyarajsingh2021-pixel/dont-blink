MISSION & METHODOLOGY

About the DON'T BLINK Cognitive Arcade

Welcome to DON'T BLINK, an independent online psychomotor benchmarking platform and web arcade created to measure, track, and improve human cognitive reaction speeds, optical discrimination, working memory capacity, and motor precision.

Our Core Mission

In modern digital environments—from esports and competitive gaming to surgical operations, driving, and high-frequency knowledge work—human sensory processing latency represents a fundamental cognitive bottleneck. Traditional psychometric testing tools are often locked behind proprietary academic software or paywalled research labs.

Our mission is to democratize high-precision psychomotor testing through accessible, lightweight, and engaging web browser experiences that run smoothly on any device without installations, paywalls, or tracking bloat.

The Science of Our Benchmarks

Each of our arcade modules isolates a specific neurological or sensory-motor pathway:

  • Simple Visual Reaction Time (DON'T BLINK): Evaluates phototransduction delay in the retinal photoreceptors, signal transmission through the optic nerve to the visual cortex, prefrontal processing, and efferent motor signal propagation to the finger muscles. Measured using the sub-millisecond precision performance.now() High Resolution Time API.
  • Optical Contrast Discrimination (Color Rush): Tests cone photoreceptor sensitivity and chromatic contrast thresholds under escalating time constraints.
  • Internal Chronometry (Perfect 10): Analyzes the human internal neural pacemaker and sub-second interval timing without external auditory or visual cues.
  • Dynamic Aim Coordination (Target Blitz): Implements Fitts's Law to benchmark ballistic spatial motor movements and peripheral visual tracking.
  • Working Memory Capacity (Chimp Memory): Replicates the Kyoto University Primate Research Institute paradigm to evaluate visuo-spatial eidetic memory and transient recall.
  • Biomechanical Motor Cadence (Typing Blitz): Gauges linguistic cognitive decoding speed, finger fluidity, and error-correction latencies under time limits.
  • Rapid Numerical Decisions (Math Sprint): Measures parietal cortex mathematical processing and working memory pressure under 2-second windows.
  • Predictive Rhythm Stacking (Stack Tower): Analyzes temporal synchronization, moving target estimation, and motor anticipation precision.

Hardware Latency & Measurement Integrity

Every digital measurement tool faces hardware latency: screen refresh cycles (e.g. 16.6ms on 60Hz displays vs 4.1ms on 240Hz displays), OS input polling rates, and browser event loop dispatch intervals. DON'T BLINK utilizes timestamp differential logging via performance.now() and direct hardware event listeners to minimize software overhead to less than 1 millisecond.

Zero Audio Bloat & Offline-First Design

We believe in high-performance web development. All audio effects across the arcade are synthesized dynamically in real-time using the Web Audio API oscillators—meaning our games load instantly, consume minimal bandwidth, and run completely offline without downloading heavy MP3/WAV files.

Editorial Standards & Team

DON'T BLINK is actively maintained by an independent group of software engineers, cognitive science enthusiasts, and web game developers. We continuously update our norms and percentiles based on aggregated anonymous gameplay data to provide the most accurate real-world benchmarks.
